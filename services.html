Our Process

Standard Operating Procedure for Every Service

We do not take shortcuts. Every air conditioner serviced by VACS undergoes a rigorous 5-step maintenance protocol:

01. Electrical Check

Testing voltage stability, starting current (Amps), earthing, and terminal connections.

02. Pressure Wash

High-pressure water descaling of both indoor cooling fin rows and outdoor condenser fan.

03. Drain Sanitization

Clearing slime, fungal buildup, and blockages in drain tray and pipe to prevent indoor water dripping.

04. Delta-T Temp Test

Calibrated digital thermometer verification of supply vs. return airflow differential temperature.
