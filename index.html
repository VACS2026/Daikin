Which Daikin AC tonnage is best for a 120 to 180 sq.ft bedroom or hall in Hyderabad?
For a standard 120 to 180 sq.ft bedroom in Hyderabad, a 1.5 Ton Daikin Inverter Split AC (such as the 5-Star FTKM50, FTKU50, JTKJ50 or 3-Star FTKL50) is ideal. For rooms up to 120 sq.ft, a 1.0 Ton AC is recommended, while large living rooms (180 to 260 sq.ft) require a 1.8 Ton or 2.0 Ton Daikin Split or Cassette AC to handle Hyderabad's 45°C–52°C summer heat.
