Frequently Asked Questions

Does this Daikin AC require an external voltage stabilizer?

Daikin Inverter ACs come with internal Stabilizer-Inside circuitry operating safely between 160V and 264V. In areas with extreme grid drops below 160V, our technicians can recommend an external stabilizer during installation.

What are the standard installation charges in Hyderabad?

Standard Split AC installation by certified VACS technicians is ₹ 1,500. Heavy-duty powder-coated outdoor wall brackets (L-brackets) are available at ₹ 750. Additional copper piping and drain pipes are charged as per actual meter consumption if required.

What is the warranty coverage?

Daikin provides a 10-Year Warranty on the Inverter Compressor and a 5-Year Comprehensive Warranty on the PCB electronic boards, indoor/outdoor coils, fan motor, and sensors when purchased through an authorized partner like Versatile Air Conditioner Systems.
