Pillar 01 • Space & Structural Engineering

Large-Capacity Single Module (7% Less Installation Space) & Reinforced Frame

Up to the 26 HP class (22, 24, 26 HP), Daikin VRV Alpha is now housed in a single compact outdoor module rather than twin combined units—reducing rooftop and service-balcony footprint by 7%.

  • ✓ Footprint Reduction (22–26 HP Class): Only 1.34 m² (1,750 mm × 765 mm) compared to 1.44 m² (1,880 mm × 765 mm) in conventional twin models.
  • ✓ Truss-Shape Anti-Wobble Frame: Strengthened frame structure resists earthquakes and high rooftop wind loads while preventing horizontal wobbling.
  • ✓ 2-Axis Screwed Vibration Control: Every structural frame member is bolted across 2 axes to minimize vibration from all angles.

Pillar 02 • Hardware + Software Synergy

High-Efficiency Scroll Compressor, Subcooling Plate HEX & VRT Smart II Control

Energy savings during actual part-load operation have been significantly elevated through coordinated hardware and software engineering:

  • ✓ High-Efficiency Scroll Compressor: Improved discharge port, optimized back-pressure control & new oil control function, and high-performance concentrated winding motor for higher ISEER.
  • ✓ Subcooling Plate Heat Exchanger: Larger heat exchange capacity than conventional double-tube designs—reducing required refrigerant volume and lowering pipe pressure loss.
  • ✓ VRT Smart II Coordinated Control: Indoor unit calculates exact capacity needed from $\Delta T$ (Room temp vs. Set temp), regulates airflow first, and coordinates target refrigerant temperature ($Te$) with the outdoor unit.
How VRT Smart II 5-Step Loop Works:
  1. Step 1: Indoor unit calculates required capacity based on $\Delta T$ (Room vs. Set temp) & trend.
  2. Step 2: Indoor unit regulates room comfort using fan speed control first.
  3. Step 3: If fan control is insufficient, IDU requests target evaporation temp ($Te$) change from ODU.
  4. Step 4: Outdoor unit determines refrigerant temp and modulates compressor speed & fan airflow.
  5. Step 5: Outdoor unit supplies refrigerant adjusted to moderate temperature—preventing over-cooling waste!

Why Execute VRV with VACS?

Our 6-Stage Turnkey VRV Execution Protocol

A VRV system is only as reliable as its copper piping, REFNET brazing, and vacuum commissioning. Under the direct technical supervision of CH. SANTHOSH, every VRV project follows Daikin Japan's strict installation standards:

01. Heat-Load & Diversity Calculation

Room-by-room thermal load analysis considering glazing, orientation, occupancy, and simultaneity factor (50%–200% connection ratio).

02. AutoCAD VRV Piping & Duct Design

Selection of exact copper pipe diameters, REFNET Y-joints/headers, condensate drain slopes, and ceiling diffuser placements aligned with your architect's false-ceiling plan.

03. Nitrogen-Purged Silver Brazing

Continuous dry-nitrogen purging during copper & REFNET joint brazing to prevent internal carbon scale formation that could clog electronic expansion valves (EEVs).

04. 400+ PSI Nitrogen Pressure Test

24 to 48-hour high-pressure nitrogen holding test across the entire refrigerant network prior to false-ceiling closure to guarantee 100% zero leaks.

05. Deep Micron Vacuum & Auto-Charge

Two-stage vacuum pump evacuation below 500 microns to remove all moisture, followed by Daikin VRV Alpha's automated refrigerant charging.

06. t01–t10 Automated Test Run & AMC

Full t01–t10 (0–99%) automated commissioning verification, CFM balancing, client handover training, and priority post-installation AMC support.
